1.Welcome

Mi Consultorio Pro is a complete system for running the operations of a clinic, independent practice, or wellness center: scheduling, patients, payments, consent forms, treatment processes and support in one place, with your own public page where patients can book 24/7.

It's built for professionals in: general medicine and specialties, dentistry and orthodontics, psychology and psychiatry, physical therapy, nutrition, functional and integrative medicine, acupuncture and traditional Chinese medicine, homeopathy, naturopathy, neural therapy, chiropractic, osteopathy, biomagnetism, bioenergetics, reiki, emotional healing and biodecoding, emotional coaching and mentoring, breathwork and meditation therapies, body therapies and massage, and wellness centers in general.

You don't need any technical knowledge. The system is designed for you to set it up yourself, with a guided step-by-step wizard. If you know how to use WhatsApp or social media, you can use Mi Consultorio Pro.

This guide describes every functionality of the system, organized the same way they appear in your dashboard menu. You don't need to read it start to finish — use it as a reference and jump to whichever module you need at any given moment. Inside your dashboard, under Help, you'll also find practical tips per module and a frequently-asked-questions list — often faster to check than this full guide.

2.Activating your account after purchase

Mi Consultorio Pro is sold as a single, complete annual plan: $39 USD per year, support included (Clinical Continuity Plan), renewed every year. There are no limited tiers or trial versions with locked features: the moment you create your account, you have access to every module described in this guide for the 12 months of your plan.

  1. Complete your purchase through Hotmart.
  2. You'll receive a welcome email with a direct link to create your account at mcpro.seitonhome.com/register.
  3. Enter your clinic's name, your name, your email, and a password.
  4. Your clinic is created immediately, with full access to every module for 1 year — you don't need to enter any code.
ImportantKeep your email and password somewhere safe. If you have front-desk or administrative staff, you can give them their own access later from your dashboard, with permissions adjusted to their role.
If your plan expires without being renewed, the system stops being operational (you can't use scheduling, patients, or payments) until you renew — but you never lose your data. You can always log in and download all of your clinic's information as a file, even with an expired plan.

3.Getting started: the setup wizard

When you create your account, the system walks you through a 12-step wizard before publishing your clinic. You can move back and forth between steps; a sidebar shows which ones you've already completed, and a percentage ("X% configured") appears at the top.

  1. Clinic details — commercial name, legal name, contact information.
  2. Logo and colors — your logo and brand colors.
  3. Type of practice — medical, dental, psychological, alternative medicine, wellness, etc. This automatically adjusts the language your patients see ("appointment" vs. "session" vs. "treatment"; "patient" vs. "client").
  4. Professionals — who provides care: you and/or your team.
  5. Locations or virtual mode — one or more physical locations, or virtual-only.
  6. Services — what you offer, with suggested templates based on your type of practice.
  7. Schedules — your availability.
  8. Payments — how you'll get paid.
  9. Consent forms — the legal texts patients accept before booking, with suggested templates based on your type of practice.
  10. Your public page — the web address where patients will find you.
  11. Test booking — a practice booking to confirm everything works as expected.
  12. Publish — you activate your clinic so the public can see it and book.

After publishing, an activation checklist on your home dashboard reminds you of useful tasks: add a logo, choose a visual theme, create your first service, set up a schedule, activate a payment method, review your data policy, publish your page, create your first appointment, share your booking link, and try the flow as if you were a patient.

TipYou don't need to finish everything at once. Activate the essentials and keep adjusting services, schedules and legal texts as you go.

4.Your brand and visual theme

From Settings → Brand you customize how your clinic looks to your patients:

  • Commercial name, editable at any time.
  • Logo, shown on your public page and, in addition, next to your clinic's name in your own dashboard's sidebar and mobile header.
  • Cover image, a banner shown at the top of your public page.
  • Featured professional photo, a portrait next to your name in the header.
  • Five ready-made visual themes, each designed for a type of practice:
    ThemeIdeal for
    Modern ClinicalGeneral medicine, specialists, psychiatry
    Premium WellnessTherapists, energy healing, coaching, holistic centers
    IntegrativeFunctional medicine, acupuncture, homeopathy
    Emotional TherapeuticPsychology, emotional therapy
    Premium DentalCosmetic dentistry, orthodontics
  • Custom theme, where you set your own primary and secondary colors.
  • Typographic style: standard, elegant, or technical.

Your theme is applied automatically across your dashboard, your public page, your booking flow, and your patient portal. Logo, cover image and photo each accept files up to 5 MB.

Publishing your page is a separate step. This same section has a button to publish or unpublish your public page — see section 9 for details.

5.Professionals

In Professionals you create, edit and deactivate the people who provide care at your clinic — this isn't something you only set up once during registration; you can come back to this module any time.

  • Name, professional type, specialty, photo and short bio.
  • Every active professional automatically appears on your public page, under "Our team," with their own individual page.
  • If a professional stops working with you, deactivate them instead of deleting — you keep their past appointment and process history, and they stop being available for new bookings.

6.Services

In Services you define exactly what you offer. Each service includes:

  • Name, description, category and duration.
  • Classification: licensed health service, alternative/complementary therapy, wellness service, educational/coaching service, or non-clinical service — this automatically adjusts language, disclaimers and suggested consent forms.
  • Price, and whether you want it shown publicly or not.
  • Payment type: no charge, deposit, full payment, manual, or in-person.
  • Modality: in-person, virtual, or both.
  • Assigned professional and location, calendar color.
  • Whether it requires an additional, service-specific consent form.
  • Minimum advance-booking time and maximum cancellation window.
  • Pre-appointment instructions and a post-appointment message.
  • A service-specific disclaimer.
  • Whether it can be sold as a session package or as a group service (with a maximum capacity).

You can activate or deactivate a service without deleting it — useful if you stop offering it temporarily.

7.Schedules and availability

In Settings → Schedules you set each professional's availability: which days and hours they work, with a configurable buffer between appointments. You can also block off specific windows (vacation, a booked-out day, an emergency). The system automatically calculates which time slots are open for booking, subtracting your existing appointments and blocks.

8.Calendar

In Calendar you manage your appointments for the day, week, or any range you choose, with filters by professional, patient and status.

  • Every appointment moves through clear statuses: requested, pending payment, pending manual confirmation, confirmed, paid, checked in, in progress, completed, cancelled, rescheduled, no-show, or expired.
  • You can confirm, reschedule, mark as "no-show", or cancel an appointment — every change is logged.
  • The system automatically prevents double-booking the same professional.
  • In-person, virtual, and (depending on your setup) group appointments.
  • Filtering by a specific patient drops the single-day view and instead shows their entire appointment history, past and future, sorted by date — no need to page through days to find their last visit.
  • An appointment created manually from "New appointment" in the dashboard sends the patient the same confirmation email they'd get from booking themselves on your public page, as long as they have an email on file.

9.Your public page and the patient booking flow

Every clinic gets its own public page (for example, mcpro.seitonhome.com/c/your-clinic), which works as a professional mini-landing page: your logo, cover image and photo, name, description, services with prices (if you choose to show them), duration, modality, address or Google Maps link, WhatsApp, and your booking button.

Your page isn't visible until you publish it. Go to Settings → Brand and confirm it says "Your public page is live." If it says it isn't published yet, click "Publish my page" — until you do, any visitor (including your own patients) will see a page-not-found error. You can unpublish it at any time if you need to pause new bookings temporarily, without deleting anything.

The patient booking flow, with no account required, is:

  1. Choose a service.
  2. Choose a professional (if you have more than one) and a location or modality.
  3. Choose an available date and time.
  4. Enter their contact details.
  5. Accept the data policy and any required consent forms.
  6. Choose a payment method, if the service requires one.
  7. Confirm the booking and receive confirmation with date, time, service, professional, address or virtual link, pre-appointment instructions, a secure link to manage their appointment (reschedule or cancel, depending on what you allow), and a direct link to their patient portal.

10.Patients

In Patients you keep the administrative record of everyone who consults you: name, ID type and number, contact information, date of birth, city, emergency contact. A search box filters by name, ID, phone or email at once. Each patient record shows, in separate tabs:

  • Their appointment history.
  • Their payments.
  • The consent forms they've accepted, with the exact date and version.
  • Their documents (uploaded securely, with download links that expire, up to 15 MB per file).
  • Administrative notes.
  • Their active packages and remaining sessions, if applicable.
ImportantWhen uploading a document to a patient's record, there's a "Clinical document" checkbox. If you check it, only your staff can see it; if you leave it unchecked, the patient can also see it in their own portal. Review it consciously every time — the system doesn't decide for you.

Administrative data is always kept separate from clinical data (see next section).

11.Protected clinical module

For professionals who need to keep private notes on each patient, the clinical module offers a separate, access-restricted space:

  • Reason for consultation, private notes, progress, recommendations and follow-up plan.
  • Additional protected files.
  • PDF export.
  • Access audit: every time someone opens a patient's clinical record, it's logged who and when.
This module is an internal record for the professional, meant to give continuity and privacy to your own follow-up — it does not replace an official, interoperable clinical history in the regulatory sense.

12.Consent forms and legal documents

In Consent you edit the legal texts your patients accept when booking: data processing policy, personal and sensitive data authorization, general informed consent, teleconsultation consent, service-specific consent, cancellation and refund policy, your clinic's terms and conditions, and disclaimers for alternative medicine, wellness, or non-clinical services.

What happens after you create the document? Nothing else needed on your end. From that point on it's shown automatically to the patient when they book (the general one, or the service-specific one if you attached it to one), and they must accept it to confirm their appointment — if they don't, they can't continue. It's a required step in booking, not an optional notice.

Every time you edit a text, the system saves a new version without losing the previous ones, and the date each patient accepted is recorded. You can check it anytime from the patient's profile (section 10), under the "Consent" tab. If you no longer need a document, you can deactivate it without deleting it: it stops being asked of new patients, but the record of who already accepted it stays.

ImportantYou are responsible for the legal content of these texts. The system gives you the tooling (versioning, proof of acceptance, suggested templates by type of practice) but does not draft or legally validate the content for you — check your obligations with an advisor if you have questions.

13.Payments center

From Payments → Settings you activate the methods you'll use to get paid. No card data is ever stored, and your payment gateway credentials are kept encrypted.

  • Bank transfer: the patient transfers and your team confirms manually.
  • In-person payment: the patient pays when they arrive.
  • Wompi: automatic payments by card, PSE and more.
  • Mercado Pago: automatic checkout.
  • ePayco: automatic checkout with card, PSE and cash.
  • External payment link: if you already have a payment link on another platform, just paste it in.
  • Every service can require a deposit or full payment, and you decide whether confirmation is automatic or manual.

PayU, Bold and PlaceToPay show up in the selector marked "coming soon" — the architecture already reserves them, but the real integration isn't built yet.

14.Payment reconciliation

In Payments → Reconciliation you review payments that need manual confirmation: patient, service, expected amount, gateway or method, external reference, and attached proof of payment (if the patient uploaded one). You can confirm, reject, or mark under review any payment — every action is logged with who did it and when.

15.Session packages

For professionals who sell full treatment processes, not just single appointments: in Packages you create a package with a name, number of sessions, total price, deposit, start date and validity period. The system automatically tracks used and remaining sessions. Statuses: active, completed, paused, expired, or cancelled. You can edit the name, session count, price and validity after creation, from the pencil icon on each package.

Registering a package session doesn't automatically create a Calendar appointment, and vice versa — they're two independent records you need to keep in sync yourself.

16.Therapeutic processes

In Processes you track an ongoing course of care with a patient: the goal of the process, number of sessions, progress, next session, private notes, tasks or recommendations, and resources sent. Statuses: active, paused, completed, abandoned, or cancelled. Only you (clinic owner) and the treating professional have access — not front-desk or admin staff.

17.Group sessions and workshops

In Groups you schedule workshops, circles, group classes, meditations, or wellness days: name, description, maximum and available spots, list of attendees, date, time, location or virtual link, and payment status per attendee. Enrollment today is managed by your staff from the dashboard (there's no public self-enrollment page yet), and the system never lets you go over the maximum capacity.

18.Waitlist

If a time slot is full, a patient can join the Waitlist with their desired service, professional and date preferences. When a spot opens up — for example, from a cancellation — you can reach out directly to whoever is on the list to offer them that opening. Your home dashboard always shows how many patients are currently on your waitlist.

19.Post-appointment follow-up

In Follow-ups you schedule communications for after an appointment: a thank-you message, a satisfaction survey, a review request, a package renewal nudge, or a custom message. Clicking "Send now" makes the system send a real email to the patient right away — no need to write it yourself over WhatsApp. If the follow-up is a review request, the email already includes the link to your public reviews page (see section 21). If the patient has no email on file, the system warns you before attempting to send.

20.Resource library

In Resources you upload PDFs, audio, video and guides (exercises, meditations, instructions, educational material), up to 25 MB per file, to send to your patients after a session. Each patient sees, from their own portal, only the resources you specifically assigned to them. Removing a resource from the library doesn't revoke access for patients who already had it assigned — it just stops being offered for new assignments.

21.Reviews

In Reviews you manage your patients' ratings and comments. Your public page includes a link ("Already visited us? Leave us a review") to a simple star-rating and comment form, with no sign-in required — you can also share that link directly over WhatsApp or include it in a post-appointment follow-up. You can also manually log a review you received through another channel (phone, WhatsApp, in person).

Every review is private by default: none show up on your public page until you approve or feature it, always checking first that it doesn't reveal sensitive information.

22.Reports

In Reports you check the last 6 months of activity: appointments per month, revenue (approved payments only), new patients, most-booked services — with clear charts and CSV export for your accountant or your own spreadsheet.

23.Business opportunity panel

In Opportunities the system surfaces practical signals to help grow your clinic, computed in real time from your own data: patients without a next appointment, cancelled slots you could recover via your waitlist, payments pending confirmation, open hours this week based on your real availability, top-demand services, frequent patients, and inactive patients (haven't visited in over 60 days). It's not automated marketing — it's an actionable summary of your own information, worth checking once a week.

24.Patient portal

Your patients have their own access, no password required: they go to mcpro.seitonhome.com/portal/login, enter their email, and receive a secure, one-time link valid for one hour. That access is already linked from their appointment confirmation email, so you don't need to explain how to get there. From their portal they can see:

  • Their next appointment and their appointment history.
  • Their active package and remaining sessions.
  • The resources you've sent them.
  • Their payments.
  • The consent forms they've accepted.
  • Their administrative documents (the ones you uploaded without checking "Clinical document" — never the clinical ones).
  • A button to reschedule or cancel, depending on what you allow.

If the same email has history at more than one of your clinics (or at other clinics that also use Mi Consultorio Pro), the patient can choose which one to view, all with a single sign-in.

26.Frequently asked questions

Is it a one-time payment or a subscription?
It's an annual plan of $39 USD, not a monthly fee: you pay once a year and it includes every module plus support (Clinical Continuity Plan). It renews every year; if you don't renew, the system pauses but your data stays available for download.

What happens if I don't renew my plan?
The system stops being operational (you can't use scheduling, patients, or payments) until you renew, but no data is ever deleted or hidden. You can log in at any time, even with an expired plan, and download all of your clinic's information.

Do I need any technical knowledge?
No. The setup wizard guides you step by step.

How do I charge my patients?
By bank transfer, in person, or automatically with Wompi, Mercado Pago, or ePayco.

Is my data and my patients' data secure?
Each clinic's data is completely isolated — no one from another clinic can see it. Clinical notes are kept separate from administrative data, with access auditing.

Does it work for alternative medicine or unconventional therapies?
Yes. The system classifies each service and adapts wording, disclaimers, and consent forms accordingly, using responsible language that doesn't replace a medical evaluation when one is needed.

Can I try it before buying?
Yes, there's a public demo mode with sample data from a real clinic.

My public page shows a "page not found" error.
This almost always means you haven't published it yet. Go to Settings → Brand and confirm it says "Your public page is live" (see section 9).

A document I uploaded for a patient doesn't show up in their portal.
Check whether you marked the "Clinical document" checkbox when uploading it — those are only visible to your staff, never to the patient (see section 10).

I uploaded a file and it said it was too large.
The limits are 15 MB for patient documents, 25 MB for digital resources, and 5 MB for brand images.

What happens if I need help after purchasing?
Check Help inside your dashboard first; if you can't find the answer, open a ticket from Support, with response times based on your priority level (see section 25).
